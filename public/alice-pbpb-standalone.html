Model & references
  • Glauber MC: Pb Woods–Saxon R = 6.62 fm, a = 0.546 fm, σNN = 68 mb, σinelPbPb = 7.67 b (PRC 97, 054910, 2018).
  • Multiplicity: two-component dN/dη = 5.1[(1−x)Npart/2 + xNcoll], x = 0.12, tuned to ≈ 2000 for 0–5% (cf. ALICE, PRL 116, 222302, 2016).
  • Flow: v₂ = 0.23 ε₂ (participant plane), pT-dependent; pT spectra Γ-shaped with mass-ordered slopes (π, K, p) plus a power-law tail.
  • Tracking: helices with R[m] = pT/(0.3·B), hits on 7 ITS2 layers and TOF. Geometry after ALICE, JINST 3 S08002 (2008) and ITS upgrade TDR, J.Phys.G 41, 087002 (2014).
  • EMCal/DCal: 20 supermodules (EMCal 10 full + 2×⅓, DCal 6×⅔ + 2×⅓), 4416 modules, 17 664 Pb–scintillator towers, Δη×Δφ ≈ 0.0143×0.0143, projective; EMCal |η|<0.7, 80°<φ<187°; DCal 0.22<|η|<0.7, 260°<φ<320° and |η|<0.7, 320°<φ<327° (ALICE EMCal page). Front face at r = 4.28 m. σE/E = 1.7% ⊕ 11.3%/√E ⊕ 4.8%/E (test beam, arXiv:1008.0413; ALICE, JINST 18, P08007, 2023).
  • Neutrals: π⁰ (≈ π⁺ yield) and η (η/π⁰ ≈ 0.11) decay to γγ; photons shower over ~3×3 towers. Charged hadrons: 62% MIP (≈ 0.27 GeV), the rest a partial hadronic shower. No PHOS.
  • Nuclei scale: drawn at true size by default (RPb ≈ 7.1 fm, far below a pixel at detector scale); the Nuclei zoom slider magnifies them, and the QGP fireball with them, up to ≈ 4.5×10¹³. Lorentz contraction drawn at γ = 12 (true γ ≈ 2.9×10³). Tch ≈ 156 MeV (Nature 561, 321, 2018).
